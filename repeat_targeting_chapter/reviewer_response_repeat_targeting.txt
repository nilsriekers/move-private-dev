Reviewer comment:
"For Fig. 7, where the authors target the 4th syllable in a repeat phrase, it is unclear
how reliably they were able to do this. Some accuracy measures along with false positive
and false negative proportions would be useful."

---

Response:

We thank the reviewer for this suggestion. To address this concern, we evaluated Moove's
targeting performance on the 12 recording bouts from which the Fig. 7 example was drawn.
We ran Moove's full inference pipeline (segmentation → classification → pattern-matching
trigger rule) on the recorded audio with parameters tuned for this bird, and assessed
trigger accuracy against manually corrected ground-truth syllable labels. A trigger was
counted as a true positive if it fell within a 10 ms collar of the ground-truth target
onset; triggers outside this window were counted as false positives, and undetected target
phrases as false negatives.

Across the 12 bouts (36 target phrases in total), Moove correctly triggered on 34 phrases
(precision: 97.1%, recall: 94.4%, F1 = 0.96). There was one false positive and two false
negatives. Both false negatives arose from the same mechanism: in unusually long repetition
runs, the first 'a' syllable was classified with low confidence, momentarily breaking the
required streak of four consecutive 'a' detections and shifting the trigger one syllable
later — beyond the acceptance collar. The single false positive was a correspondingly
shifted trigger from the same mechanism. These results demonstrate that Moove reliably
identifies the target position within repeat phrases.

We have added a quantitative statement to the revised manuscript (see updated Fig. 7
section) and clarified the caption wording to make the trigger position (a4) and the
targeted syllable (a5) explicit.
